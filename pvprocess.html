Process

制作の流れ

From first conversation to final file — here is how we work.

お問い合わせから納品まで、5つのステップでプロジェクトを進めます。

STEP 01

Inquiry

お問い合わせ・ヒアリング

We start by understanding your goals, audience, and vision — so every creative decision that follows is grounded in what actually matters for your project.

ご希望の映像イメージや目的、スケジュールをヒアリングします。

GoalsTarget audienceScheduleLocationDeliverables

STEP 02

Creative Planning

企画・構成

We translate your brief into a visual plan — moodboard, storyboard, shot list, and creative direction. You see exactly what we're building before a single frame is shot.

ブランドイメージに合わせた映像演出や撮影構成をご提案します。

MoodboardStoryboardShot listCreative direction

STEP 03

Production

撮影本番

Our team handles everything on set — directing, lighting, camera operation, and timing. We move efficiently without sacrificing the quality of each shot.

現場ディレクションから撮影まで、一貫して進行します。

DroneLightingCameraOn-set direction

STEP 04

Post Production

編集・仕上げ

This is where the footage becomes a film. We cut for pace and emotion, grade for mood, design the sound, and add any subtitles or graphics your project needs.

編集・カラーグレーディング・音楽選定を通して映像を仕上げます。

EditingColor gradingSound designSubtitles

STEP 05

Delivery

納品

Final files delivered in the formats you need — optimised for social, broadcast, or web. We don't hand over a single file and disappear.

用途に合わせた形式で納品いたします。

SNS formatsVertical editsMultilingual versions
